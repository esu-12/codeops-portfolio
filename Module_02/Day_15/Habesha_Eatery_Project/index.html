Our Story

Habesha Eatery has been serving authentic Ethiopian cuisine in Addis Ababa with passion and tradition. Every meal is prepared using fresh local ingredients and time-honored recipes that bring families and friends together.

Whether you're enjoying our famous Doro Wat, flavorful Tibs, or delicious vegetarian dishes, we strive to make every visit memorable with warm Ethiopian hospitality.
